Our Mission

The Pathfinder Club is a church-centered spiritual-recreational-activity program designed for young people ages 10-15. We aim to develop Christian character, leadership skills, and a love for God and nature through hands-on activities and community service.

Our mission is to help every Pathfinder:

  • Develop a personal relationship with Jesus Christ
  • Build strong character and moral values
  • Learn practical life and survival skills
  • Develop leadership abilities
  • Serve their community with compassion

Our Vision

We envision a generation of young people who are:

  • Spiritually grounded in their faith
  • Physically active and healthy
  • Mentally sharp and curious learners
  • Socially responsible and compassionate
  • Prepared to make positive contributions to society

Through adventure, service, and spiritual growth, we strive to equip Pathfinders with the tools they need to navigate life's challenges and become leaders in their communities.

Our Core Values

✝️

Spiritual Growth

Growing in faith and relationship with God through Bible study, worship services, prayer groups, and spiritual mentorship. We create an environment where young people can explore their faith and build a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

💪

Physical Development

Developing healthy bodies and outdoor skills through camping, hiking, sports, and wilderness adventures. We believe in the importance of physical fitness and teaching practical survival skills that build confidence and self-reliance.

🧠

Mental Excellence

Encouraging learning and critical thinking through honor classes, academic challenges, and skill development. We foster curiosity, creativity, and a lifelong love of learning in every Pathfinder.

❤️

Social Responsibility

Building friendships and serving others through community service projects, teamwork activities, and compassionate outreach. We teach Pathfinders the joy of giving back and making a positive impact in their communities.

Our Programs

🏕️

Camping & Outdoor Skills

Adventure & Survival Training

Learn essential outdoor skills including tent setup, fire building, hiking, wilderness survival, orienteering, and camping safety. Our experienced leaders guide Pathfinders through hands-on outdoor adventures that build confidence and self-reliance.

  • Monthly camping trips
  • Wilderness survival training
  • Hiking and backpacking
  • Navigation and orienteering
  • Outdoor cooking
🤝

Community Service

Making a Difference

Make a difference through service projects that help our local community and develop leadership skills. From food banks to park cleanups, Pathfinders learn the joy of serving others and making a positive impact.

  • Food bank volunteering
  • Park and trail maintenance
  • Senior center visits
  • Charity fundraisers
  • Homeless outreach programs
🏆

Honor Classes

Skills & Achievement

Earn honors in areas like nature study, arts and crafts, health and science, and outdoor industries. Our comprehensive honor program encourages Pathfinders to explore their interests and develop new skills while working toward achievement badges.

  • Nature and conservation honors
  • Arts, crafts, and hobbies
  • Health and science
  • Vocational and life skills
  • Spiritual growth honors
📖

Spiritual Development

Faith Formation

Grow in faith through Bible study, worship services, prayer groups, and spiritual mentorship. We create a supportive environment where young people can explore their faith and build a personal relationship with God.

  • Weekly devotional time
  • Bible study groups
  • Youth worship services
  • Prayer and meditation
  • One-on-one mentorship
